1. Information We Collect

Photos & Visual Data

When you use the App to upload a photo for AI redesign (e.g., before-and-after transformations), we temporarily process and image on our servers to generate results. Images may be stored securely for a limited time to improve AI performance or user experience (e.g., history view), unless you opt out.

User-Provided Information

  • Name or username (optional)
  • Email address (if you register or contact support)
  • Feedback or support requests

Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage analytics to understand how users interact with the App. This may include:

  • Device type and OS
  • App usage patterns
  • Crash logs

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI before-and-after redesigns.
  • To personalize and improve the user experience.
  • To respond to user inquiries or support requests.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve App performance.
  • To send occasional service-related notifications (if enabled).

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data:

  • With cloud providers for AI processing (e.g. image generation).
  • With service providers helping us operate and improve the App (under confidentiality agreements).
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

4. Your Privacy Choices

  • Image Retention: You can delete your redesigns at any time from your app history.
  • Email Communication: You may opt out of non-essential emails.
  • Data Access/Deletion: You may request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us at info@appdesigen.com.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, including encryption during transfer and secure cloud storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Desigen is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

7. International Users

If you access the App from outside your home country, your data may be processed in jurisdictions with different data protection laws.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll notify you of material changes via the App or email.

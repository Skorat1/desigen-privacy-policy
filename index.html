1. Information We Collect

Information We Collect

Easy Art Studio does not require user login or signup.

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Password
  • Location data

Users can use the app freely without creating an account.

Automatically Collected Information

Easy Art Studio does not directly collect:

  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • Personal user data

However, the app displays advertisements using Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information such as:

Advertising ID

Basic device information (for ad delivery and fraud prevention)

App interaction data related to ads

This information is collected and processed directly by Google according to their Privacy Policy.

You can review Google’s Privacy Policy here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

2. How We Use Your Information

Easy Art Studio may request the following permissions:

Storage Permissions

  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (up to Android 12)
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (up to Android 12)
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+)

These permissions are used only to:

  • Save user-created drawings to the device
  • Allow users to access saved images

We do not access or collect unrelated files from your device.

Wallpaper Permissions

  • SET_WALLPAPER

This permission is used to:

  • Allow users to set their created drawings as device wallpaper.

We do not modify wallpaper without user action.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.
